Last updated April 09, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Apiant, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website at https://apiant.com or any website of ours that links to this policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give website owners useful information.

How can I control cookies?

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

Browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete existing cookies, and set preferences for certain websites. The following links explain how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Chrome
  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge

Opt-out of targeted advertising. You can opt out of targeted advertising through the following industry programs:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance
  • Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

Opt-out of specific services:

  • Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Smartlook: Visit Smartlook's opt-out page
  • Facebook: Adjust your Facebook Ad Preferences

Please note that refusing or removing cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Other tracking technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use web beacons (also known as "tracking pixels" or "clear gifs") and similar technologies. These are small graphics that contain a unique identifier, allowing us to recognize when someone has visited our website or opened an email. This helps us monitor traffic patterns, measure the effectiveness of email campaigns, and improve site performance.

Do we serve targeted advertising?

Third parties may serve cookies on your device to deliver advertising through our website. These companies may use information about your visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertisements about goods and services you may be interested in. The information collected through this process does not enable us or them to identify you by name or contact details unless you choose to provide these.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please revisit this policy regularly to stay informed. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated.
