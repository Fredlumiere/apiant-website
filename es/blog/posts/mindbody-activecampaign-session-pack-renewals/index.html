Triggering ActiveCampaign Renewal Emails When Session Packs Drop Below 3

Fred Lumiere Apr 15, 2025 Lectura de 5 minutos
#Fitness y Bienestar#MenteCuerpo#CampañaActiva#CRMConnect#Sincronización bidireccional

Class packs and session packs are the unloved children of fitness CRM. Memberships have auto-pay. Drop-ins are obvious one-shots. Packs are the awkward middle: a client buys 10 sessions, burns through them at one a week, and somewhere around session 8 the trainer is supposed to “have the renewal conversation.” Half the time the trainer forgets. The other half, the trainer remembers two sessions too late and the client takes a three-week gap that turns into an indefinite one.

Hypothetical: North Ridge Strength (illustrative), a single-location personal training studio with 6 trainers and 180 clients on session packs, ran a manual audit and found that roughly 22 percent of their pack clients let their last pack lapse and never renewed. Most of those clients had not consciously decided to quit; they just stopped showing up because the cadence broke.

The trainers were not lazy. They were just trying to coach clients while also being the renewal-tracking system. That role does not scale past about 30 clients per trainer.

What CRMConnect Syncs Each Night

CRMConnect Mindbody to ActiveCampaign keeps remaining sessions and pack status updated continuously on the ActiveCampaign contact. From the app page: “Automatically track each client’s membership status, expiration dates, and remaining sessions to effortlessly manage renewals and upsells.” Profiles, bookings, and visits sync instantly. The appointment and sales pipelines refresh every 15 minutes; daily visits reconcile once per day.

The relevant fields on each contact, populated automatically:

  • remaining_sessions (entero, se actualiza después de cada visita)
  • pricing_option_name (p. ej., “Entrenamiento Personal para 10 personas”)
  • pricing_option_expiration_date
  • last_visit_date, last_visit_staff, last_visit_service
  • membership_status (Activo, Vencido, Suspendido, etc.)
  • total_visits

Because remaining_sessions updates within seconds of a Mindbody visit, you can build automation around a precise threshold rather than a polling-based estimate.

A typical pack-client record on ActiveCampaign looks like:

{
  "email": "client@example.com",
  "pricing_option_name": "10-Pack Personal Training",
  "remaining_sessions": 2,
  "pricing_option_expiration_date": "2026-06-30",
  "last_visit_date": "2026-05-16",
  "last_visit_staff": "Mira",
  "membership_status": "Active",
  "total_visits": 38
}

Building the Threshold Automation in ActiveCampaign

In ActiveCampaign (the conditional logic needs Plus or higher), build one automation enrolled on remaining_sessions drops below 3 AND membership_status = Active.

Touch 1: same day, evening. A short email written like a text from the trainer. “Hey [first name], you’re at [remaining_sessions] sessions left in your [pricing_option_name]. Want me to drop the next pack on your account so we don’t miss a week?” Personalize the sender to last_visit_staff so it reads as personal.

Touch 2: 48 hours later if remaining_sessions is still > 0 and unchanged. Slightly stronger nudge with a one-click renewal link.

Touch 3: same day as final session. “Last one in the pack today, see you at [appointment time].” Use the next_scheduled_appointment field. This email matters because it normalizes the renewal conversation in person; the trainer knows the client just got an email about it, so the in-person ask is no longer awkward.

Touch 4: 3 days after pack expiration if no new pack purchased. Soft re-engagement, possibly with a small bonus session offer.

Layer one safety: if last_visit_date is more than 14 days agoSalta los contactos del 1 al 3 y ve directamente a recuperar al cliente. La estrategia de renovación no funciona con un cliente que ya se está alejando; primero necesitas restablecer el hábito.

Personalizing the Offer Per Trainer

One of the underused tricks: the renewal email should be branded to the trainer, not the studio. Clients have a relationship with Mira or with Tomás, not with “North Ridge Strength.”

Build per-trainer email templates and use ActiveCampaign’s dynamic content rules keyed off last_visit_staffCada entrenador tiene su propio nombre, su propia firma e idealmente su propia oferta especial ("la primera sesión del nuevo paquete corre por mi cuenta"). El entrenador no tiene que hacer nada; el sistema se encarga de todo.

The Mindbody Appointment Pipeline that CRMConnect creates in ActiveCampaign (from the app page: “Automatically create a live appointment pipeline in ActiveCampaign, clearly tracking booking statuses, schedule changes, and remaining sessions”) also gives the studio manager a clean weekly view of who is approaching pack expiry. The manager can spot patterns: “Mira’s clients renew at 75 percent; Tomás’s renew at 45 percent. Let’s pair them for a week.”

Representative Renewal Lift at 180 Clients

In a representative deployment at studios of this scale, the pack-renewal rate (defined as a new pack purchased within 7 days of the previous pack’s last session) typically moves from a baseline of 60 to 70 percent into the 80 to 88 percent range within 90 days. The biggest single contributor is touch 1, the personalized “want me to drop the next pack” email; it converts because it offers a frictionless next step.

At 180 clients running roughly one pack every 8 weeks, the studio sees about 75 to 100 renewals per month. A 15-point renewal-rate lift recovers roughly 11 to 15 clients per month who would otherwise drift. At $600 per 10-pack (illustrative), that is somewhere around $7,000 to $9,000 of monthly revenue recovered, or roughly $90K annualized off one automation.

These numbers are illustrative; pack pricing and average pack-to-renewal interval will dominate the actual outcome.

Builder Takeaway

  • What to copy: the < 3 remaining sessions threshold and the trainer-personalized sender. Generic studio-branded emails convert at roughly half the rate of trainer-branded ones in this context.
  • What to watch for: remaining_sessions updates near real time, but the source-of-truth is Mindbody, not ActiveCampaign; do not let staff manually “decrement” sessions in either system. Also, ActiveCampaign’s deal pipeline features and the richer automation triggers need Plus tier or higher. SMS reminders need Professional.
  • When this pattern doesn’t fit: membership-only studios with no pack offerings get nothing from this. Studios with very large packs (50+ sessions) have a slower urgency cadence and need to shift the trigger threshold higher (probably 5 or even 8 remaining).
