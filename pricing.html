Frequently asked questions

What is a connection?

A connection is a set of credentials into one of your customer's accounts. For example, if you connect to a customer's HubSpot account, that counts as one connection. Each unique customer account you integrate with uses one connection.

Can I start with Sandbox and upgrade later?

Yes. The Sandbox plan gives you full access to the platform in a sandbox environment. Build and test your integrations, and upgrade to Pro or Scale when you're ready to go live. All your work carries over.

What does "white-label" mean?

White-label means you can brand the platform as your own. Your customers see your company name, logo, and domain. APIANT runs invisibly in the background. Available on Pro plans and above.

How does the 14-day trial work?

Start a Pro or Scale trial with full access to production features. No charge until the trial ends. Cancel anytime during the trial period and you won't be billed.

What if I need more than 50 connections?

On Pro and Scale plans, you can add connections for $20/month each. For high-volume needs, Enterprise plans include unlimited connections with volume pricing. Contact us to discuss what works best for your scale.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Switch to annual billing and save 20% on Pro and Scale plans. Use the toggle at the top of this page to see annual pricing.
