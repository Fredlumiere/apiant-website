Kluczowe funkcje of Cliniko + HubSpot Integracja

Automatycznie turn Cliniko data into actionable insights and automatyzacjas within HubSpot, streamlining your operations and enhancing pacjent relationships.

Dwukierunkowy Patient Profile Sync

Automatycznie keep Cliniko pacjent profiles and HubSpot kontakts perfectly synced in w czasie rzeczywistym. Demographics, preferences, wizyta history, and 120+ key properties flow between platformas so your team always works with accurate data.

Next Wizyta Sync

Always know when pacjents are scheduled or overdue for their next visit. Wizyta details sync automatycznie into HubSpot, enabling reminders, no-show recovery, and gentle recall nudges for inactive pacjents.

Potok faktur Cliniko

Dlaczego ta aplikacja API jest lepsza niż narzędzia DIY, takie jak Make, Appy Pie czy Pipedream?
This API App is a turnkey, professionally-engineered integracja solution built by experts with deep domain knowledge in both applications.

Unlike DIY tools, which leave you to build, debug, and manage integracjas yourself, our integracja is pre-built, thoroughly tested, and includes sophisticated logic that ensures data flows accurately, reliably, and on-time.

It’s configurable, so you can tailor how your apps communicate without coding. Plus, you get VIP klient wsparcie from specialists who understand your specific business needs. Within minutes, your systems are fully connected - no technical headaches, no trial-and-error.

Integracjas aren’t side-projects; they’re critical software infrastructure. Trust them to professionals who've perfected it through years of experience.
How does the cennik compare to DIY integracja tools like Make, Appy Pie or Pipedream?
It ultimately depends on how much data you’ll sync between your apps. DIY integracja tools typically charge per "task" performed - these costs add up quickly, especially when your integracja demands sophisticated logic, transformacja danychs (like dates or currencies), caching, and complex przepływ pracys.

Our API Apps, however, are built with advanced funkcje and integracjas that simply can't be replicated using these DIY tools.

Price-wise, our solutions offer a clear advantage: at your selected usage level (e.g., number of klients), we often allow you to sync unlimited records, including dane klientów, sales, transactions, and more. This makes our API Apps not only superior in capability but also far more cost-effective as your needs scale.
How does the dwukierunkowy klient sync work?
The sync from Cliniko → HubSpot is automatic. The sync from HubSpot → Cliniko is triggered by a custom property in HubSpot called "Push to Cliniko" which natychmiastowo pushes the kontakt to Cliniko as a pacjent or a kontakt (your choice). If a matching pacjent or kontakt already exists, an update is made. If not, a pacjent is created in Cliniko. The "Push to Cliniko" custom property can also be set by a form or a przepływ pracy in HubSpot.
Czy muszę utworzyć właściwości w HubSpot, aby otrzymywać dane z Cliniko?
Nie. Tworzenie właściwości w HubSpot odbywa się automatycznie po instalacji.
Are wizytas, cases, invoices and payments synchronizacja dwukierunkowaed?
No. Wizytas, cases, invoices and payments are only synced from Cliniko to HubSpot.
How much historical data is included with the integracja?
One month of wizytas and invoices are synced upon install. More historical transactions can be synced upon request for an additional cost.
Którego HubSpot Hub potrzebuję do uruchomienia CRMConnect?
CRMConnect will work with the free version of HubSpot, but some funkcje and benefits, such as Wizyta and Sales pipeline require a HubSpot Pro Hub which also allows przepływ pracys. To set up even more powerful automatyzacjas, a subscription to HubSpot’s Sales Hub Enterprise allows for auto-enrolling kontakts in sequences. This is one of the most powerful funkcje CRMConnect enables.
Are HubSpot przepływ pracys and sequences included?
No. CRMConnect only interprets and syncs data between Cliniko and HubSpot. This data then becomes very actionable, but it is up to you to build przepływ pracys, sequences, etc. If you are looking for help with this, please inquire within, as we can refer you to a HubSpot partner that is familiar with CRMConnect.
What happens to a Cliniko pacjent with no email?
A Cliniko pacjent with no email that’s synced with HubSpot will not have an email in HubSpot either.
Jak uzyskać raport ROI dla reklam?
Jeśli masz HubSpot Marketing Hub na poziomie przedsiębiorstwa, możesz przejść do Marketing / Ads, połączyć swoje konta reklamowe i skonfigurować raporty.
