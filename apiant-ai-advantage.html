The Landscape

Everyone Has AI-Built Integrations Now

Most major integration platforms are now using AI to build integrations from natural language. Zapier, Workato, MuleSoft, Boomi, SAP, SnapLogic, and dozens of well-funded startups all demo some version of this. That capability alone is no longer differentiating.

Here is what makes APIANT
fundamentally different

The ability to close the loop.

Most platforms built on code execution, then bolted AI on top. Their AI generates integrations, but code-based architectures make it significantly harder to inspect them mid-execution, mutate state to test alternate paths, diagnose failures from the inside, autonomously fix what broke, and improve the integration over time at any meaningful depth. To get there, they would likely need to rebuild from the ground up. As of today, no platform we are aware of has shipped this capability.
