04 · How We Price This

Integration capacity, not seats or tasks.

01

Price on capacity, not consumption.

You get predictable monthly cost tied to how much production integration you actually run. No per-seat tax, no per-task meter.

02

A connection is not a unit of value.

One connection can be low-value on a small customer and high-value on a large one. Tiered capacity fits better than overage pricing.

03

Software license and infrastructure stay separate.

AWS is a pass-through at cost. The software value stays on the APIANT license, cleanly.

05 · Draft Contract Language

One line covers the cap and upgrade rule.

Included Connections means active production connections on the PROD environment. DEV and test connections do not count. If Included Connections are exceeded, Customer upgrades to the next tier before enabling additional production connections.
