# SEO Indexing Baseline — 2026-06-15

Source: Google Search Console API, property `sc-domain:apiant.com` (Domain property, siteOwner). Window: 2026-03-17 to 2026-06-15 (90 days). Related: GitHub epic #31, issue #23.

## Headline

The site submits roughly **39,000-49,000 URLs** but only **4,593 distinct pages earned a single impression** in 90 days. That means **~85-90% of submitted URLs are effectively invisible** in search. The dominant cause is a **39,204-page programmatic `/connections/<App>` template farm** that is almost entirely unindexed, compounded by a **messy sitemap registration** (dead 404 sitemaps still registered, duplicate host variants).

- Total: **81,218 impressions / 517 clicks** (90 days), site-wide CTR ~0.6%.

## What the 39,204 URLs actually are

100% `/connections/<App>` programmatic template pages (e.g. `/connections/360Alumni`, `/connections/ActiveCampaign`), generated server-side from `servletTemplateConnections.html`. One thin page per app in the catalog. This is the bulk of the indexable surface and the bulk of the not-indexed problem: thin, templated, near-duplicate pages at scale, which Google declines to index without strong uniqueness/linking signals.

Note: the ~2,900 localized pages (145 × 20 languages) are NOT their own `<loc>` entries in any sitemap; they are referenced only via hreflang alternates inside `/sitemap.xml`.

## Confirmed issues feeding the sprint

1. **Sitemap sprawl (#24).** 4 dead/404 sitemaps still registered + duplicate http/www variants generate errors and mixed crawl signals. Clean to one canonical index.
2. **Thin programmatic farm (#27, escalated).** 39,204 `/connections/*` pages, ~92% unindexed. Strategic decision needed: prune, differentiate, or accept and de-prioritize in the sitemap to protect crawl budget.
3. **Blog migration not clean.** 24 legacy `blog.apiant.com` URLs still indexed/ranking; 301s incomplete or unprocessed.
4. **Blog underperforms.** ~72 `/blog/` URLs visible of 102 posts + categories. (NOTE: meta descriptions are NOT missing. Issue #26 was closed invalid — a false-negative in the audit grep, which assumed `name="description" content=` order; the generator emits `content=... name="description"`. All 103 posts have unique meta descriptions.)
5. **72 warnings on sitemap0.xml** — cause not yet diagnosed (likely noindex/redirect/blocked URLs listed in the sitemap).
6. **Extra live subdomains** in the index: `help.apiant.com`, `info.apiant.com` — confirm intentional and non-duplicative.

## Recommended sequence

1. #24 sitemap cleanup (remove dead + duplicate sitemaps; fix 72 warnings; settle on one canonical index). Quick, high-leverage.
2. #27 `/connections/*` strategy + blog migration 301 cleanup (the big crawl-budget lever).
3. #32 blog 301 migration (HubSpot redirects) once content parity is reached.

## Method note
There is no API to force general pages into the index. Indexing is earned via clean sitemaps, page quality/uniqueness, internal linking, and manual Request-Indexing for priority pages. GSC is diagnosis only.
