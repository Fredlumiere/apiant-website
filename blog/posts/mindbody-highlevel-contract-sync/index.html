Killing the Sunday Contract Reconciliation With Mindbody Contracts in HighLevel

Fred Lumiere Mar 11, 2025 5 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#HighLevel#CRMConnect#Data Engine

The Weekly Tax of Manual Contract Reconciliation

Personal training studios live and die on contracts. The contract holds the auto-pay terms, the session count, the renewal date, and the cancellation clause. It is the only document that matters at retention time. And in most studios, it lives as a Mindbody PDF and a HighLevel custom field that some human has to keep in sync by hand.

Hypothetical: Bedrock Performance (illustrative) is a single-location studio with eight trainers and 220 clients on contract-based memberships. Every Sunday night, the owner spends two hours opening Mindbody contracts and updating HighLevel custom fields: renewal date, sessions remaining, auto-pay status. Two hours, every week, in perpetuity. And by Tuesday the data is already stale because three clients used sessions on Monday.

Why Off-the-Shelf Tools Fail Here

Contracts are not a flat object. A Mindbody contract has nested terms: billing schedule, session credits, autopay attempts, scheduled changes, and termination conditions. A generic Zap can pull the top-level fields, but the autopay status (current, retrying, failed, suspended) is the field that actually matters and it sits on a child object that most builds skip.

The reconciliation problem also has a math problem: sessions remaining is purchased - used - scheduled. The integration has to track usage in real time, not just at the moment the contract is signed.

The APIANT Approach

CRMConnect Mindbody to HighLevel includes a dedicated Contract Management Sync along with the broader Membership and Services Sync. The integration treats contracts as their own object, with:

  • Membership and Services Sync keeping memberships, expiration dates, remaining sessions, and contract details aligned via dedicated HighLevel custom objects.
  • Contract Management Sync for the contract-level fields specifically.
  • Sales and Custom Object Sync for the underlying payment events so the autopay state stays current.

The unified data processing engine maps Mindbody’s nested contract payload into HighLevel custom object records cleanly. Each contract is its own record, linked to the parent contact. Renewal dates, autopay states, and session balances all flow through as live fields.

What Contract Sync Pushes to HighLevel

The contract custom object in HighLevel carries the fields that drive every retention workflow:

HighLevel custom object: Mindbody Contract
- contract_id
- contact_id (links to HighLevel contact)
- mindbody_contract_name        e.g. "12-Month PT Unlimited"
- contract_start_date
- contract_end_date
- contract_status               active|pending_cancel|expired|terminated
- autopay_status                current|retrying|failed|suspended
- autopay_next_charge_date
- autopay_last_attempt_at
- sessions_purchased
- sessions_used
- sessions_scheduled
- sessions_remaining            (computed live)
- renewal_window_opens_at       (60 days before end)

The sessions_remaining field is the one that ends the Sunday ritual. It updates in real time as sessions are booked and used. The owner does not have to open Mindbody anymore.

Designing Renewal Nudges From Contract Fields

The renewal flow runs against the custom object directly:

  1. Trigger: contract_end_date - now() <= 60 days AND contract_status = active.
  2. First touch (Day -60): Email from the head trainer with the renewal offer and a personal note.
  3. Sessions check (Day -45): If sessions_remaining > 8, send a “use it” reminder with a booking link.
  4. Personal touch (Day -30): Auto-create a HighLevel task for the assigned trainer to have an in-person renewal conversation at the next session.
  5. Soft urgency (Day -14): Email with the new contract terms attached.
  6. Final reminder (Day -3): SMS, “Your contract renews on Friday. Want to chat before then?”
  7. Suppress on renewal completion: when a new contract is created in Mindbody, the old contract’s status flips and the flow exits.

The trainer task on Day -30 is the part that converts. The automation flags it, the trainer has the conversation in the gym, and the renewal lands. No one is chasing PDF status reports anymore.

Catching Auto-Pay Failures Before They Compound

The autopay status changes are an immediate trigger:

  1. Trigger: autopay_status changes to failed or retrying.
  2. Same day: SMS to the client, “Card on file did not go through. Quick update here:” with the HighLevel update-card link.
  3. 24 hours later: Email to the assigned trainer to mention it at the next session.
  4. 48 hours later: If still failed, escalate to the owner for a personal call.
  5. Recovery: When autopay flips back to current, suppress the escalation and send a confirmation.

The same-day SMS is the difference between a 24-hour recovery and a churned client. The data has to be live for that to work. Pulling a weekly payment report does not.

Representative Hours Saved at 220 Clients

Illustrative composites, not a real customer:

  • Owner hours per week on contract reconciliation: roughly 2 hours, fully reclaimed.
  • Trainer time spent looking up “how many sessions does Sarah have left”: eliminated, because it is on the contact record.
  • Autopay failure recovery rate within 7 days (manual end-of-month process): roughly 35 to 45 percent.
  • Autopay failure recovery rate with same-day SMS: a lift into the 65 to 78 percent range.
  • Contract renewal rate (60-day window): a lift in the 5 to 9 percentage-point range, mostly from the Day -30 trainer-conversation prompt.

The numbers move with your contract pricing and trainer culture. The mechanism does not.

Builder Takeaway

  • Use a dedicated custom object for contracts. Cramming contract fields onto the contact record makes multi-contract clients unmanageable.
  • The autopay status field is the highest-value sync. Same-day failure recovery is impossible without it.
  • Use automation to flag the renewal conversation, then let a human have it. The 60-30-3 cadence works because Day -30 is a human moment.
