Showing Sales Reps a Lead's First Visit Inside Zoho CRM, Powered by Mindbody

Fred Lumiere Dec 12, 2024 4 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#Zoho CRM#CRMConnect#Bi-Directional Sync

Why Reps Stop Calling Leads After Trial Day

A boutique gym with a sales-rep-driven membership model lives by trial visits. The lead walks in, takes one class, and leaves. That single visit is the conversion moment, and the closing call needs to land within 24 to 48 hours. After 72 hours the lead has signed up for a competitor or stopped responding entirely.

Hypothetical: Quartzite Strength Studio (illustrative) is a 480-member single-location studio with three sales reps in Zoho CRM. Reps are great at qualification and great at the close. Where they break down is in the middle: they have no idea, from their Zoho dashboard, whether the lead actually showed up for the trial class. They are asking the front desk every morning, getting a half-remembered answer, and dropping leads they should be closing.

Why Off-the-Shelf Tools Fail at This

Mindbody visit data is straightforward to query, but the reps need it in Zoho CRM, not in a Mindbody report. A generic glue tool can push visit events, but most builds drop the last_visit_at and visit_count fields on the wrong record, or update the lead in batch overnight (which means the rep sees the visit on Wednesday for a Monday trial).

The reps need two things: the visit to land on the right lead, and it to land within minutes, not days.

The APIANT Approach

CRMConnect Mindbody to Zoho CRM is built on the two features that solve this exactly:

  • Two-Way Client Profile Sync keeps Mindbody clients and Zoho CRM contacts/leads aligned in both directions with real-time updates.
  • Last-Visit Tracking automatically tracks each client’s most recent visit so reps can follow up at the right moment.

Both push into Zoho CRM in real time. The trial-visit moment lands on the lead record before the rep finishes their morning coffee.

What Last-Visit Tracking Pushes to Zoho CRM

The lead record carries the fields that drive the rep’s day:

Zoho CRM Lead fields populated by CRMConnect:
- mindbody_client_id
- mindbody_first_visit_at
- mindbody_last_visit_at
- mindbody_visit_count
- mindbody_last_visit_class_type     e.g. "Strength 101"
- mindbody_last_visit_coach          e.g. "Coach Reeves"
- mindbody_last_visit_location
- trial_visit_completed              true|false
- trial_visit_completed_at

The trial_visit_completed_at field flips from null to a timestamp the moment the trial class attendance is recorded. That is the rep’s trigger to call.

Designing the Post-Trial Call Cadence

The Zoho CRM workflow rule is simple:

  1. Trigger: trial_visit_completed_at changes from null to a timestamp.
  2. Same day: Create a high-priority call task assigned to the lead’s owner, due within 24 hours.
  3. Task note auto-populated: “Trial class on [date], coached by [coach], format [class_type]. Talked to coach? Confirm experience and offer membership.”
  4. 24 hours later: If task still open, escalate to the sales manager with a Slack notification (via Zoho Flow).
  5. 48 hours later: If still no contact, fire a personalized email from the lead owner (Zoho CRM mail merge using mindbody_last_visit_coach as the merge field).
  6. 72 hours later: Lead status auto-changes to “Cold Trial” for the weekly nurture sequence.

The rep stops asking the front desk. The call task tells them who to call, when, and what to reference. The escalation prevents the lead from dying in someone’s inbox.

Layering Membership Sync for the Closing Call

The closing call is more effective when the rep has the actual price comparison ready. CRMConnect also syncs membership history into Zoho CRM, so the rep can see if the lead is on a previous trial, lapsed from an old membership, or new to the brand:

Additional fields from membership sync:
- mindbody_membership_status         active|trial|expired|none
- mindbody_previous_memberships      list of past memberships
- mindbody_lifetime_value            sum of all purchases
- mindbody_days_since_first_visit    int

A lead who completed a trial six months ago and let it lapse gets a different pitch than a brand-new walk-in. The rep can see it. The pitch lands.

Representative Trial-to-Sale Rate at 480 Members

Illustrative composites, not a real customer:

  • Trial-to-membership baseline (manual front-desk hand-off): roughly 22 to 30 percent.
  • Trial-to-membership with real-time visit visibility and automated call task: a lift into the 32 to 42 percent range.
  • Time from trial visit to first rep contact: cut from 24 to 48 hours to under 12 hours on most days.
  • “Did this lead actually come in?” Slack messages between reps and front desk: down sharply, because the field is on the record.

The lift is from removing the lag, not from a better pitch. The pitch is the same. The timing is the difference.

Builder Takeaway

  • The trial_visit_completed_at field is the conversion trigger. Build the call task workflow on its state change, not on calendar dates.
  • Auto-populate the task note with the coach name and class type. Reps who reference the specifics convert better than reps who open with generic small talk.
  • Escalate at 24 hours, not at 72. The lead is still warm at 24. By 72 you are saving leads instead of closing them.
