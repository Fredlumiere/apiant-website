Unifying Mindbody Enrollments, Courses, and Appointments in HighLevel

Fred Lumiere May 18, 2026 5 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#HighLevel#CRMConnect#Data Engine

Why Dojos Have Three Scheduling Surfaces

A martial arts academy almost always runs three scheduling models in parallel:

  • Enrollments for the kids’ or beginner programs (an 8-week intro that the family commits to upfront).
  • Courses for advanced curricula (a fixed-date 12-week competition prep block).
  • Appointments for private lessons, gradings, and evaluations.

Mindbody supports all three, but they live in different surfaces of the platform. When you push to a CRM, most integrations grab one (usually classes) and drop the others on the floor. The result: the HighLevel automation that thinks it knows the student’s schedule actually has a third of the picture.

Hypothetical: River Bend Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (illustrative) is a single-dojo BJJ academy with 180 students. They run beginner enrollments, advanced courses, and private lessons. The owner sends scheduling reminders out of HighLevel and keeps getting “I have a private with Professor Marco at 5:30, why are you reminding me about the 6pm class?” replies. The reminders are running on partial data.

Why Off-the-Shelf Tools Miss This

Mindbody’s APIs for enrollments, courses, and appointments are separate. A glue tool that only listens for class events skips enrollments entirely. A tool that listens for appointment events misses course-style multi-week bookings. Building all three pipelines yourself is doable, but each one has its own rate limit accounting and its own edge cases (course attendance vs. course completion, enrollment dropouts vs. transfers, private lesson reschedules).

The integration matters because the student sees a single schedule. The CRM should too.

The APIANT Approach

CRMConnect Mindbody to HighLevel includes coverage for all three scheduling surfaces:

  • Class and Appointment Calendar Sync for class bookings, appointments, enrollments, and courses, all flowing into HighLevel calendars with accurate next-booking tracking.
  • Enrollment and Course Booking Sync as a dedicated feature so the multi-week commitments are first-class records.
  • Comprehensive Visit and Activity Sync so attendance against all three surfaces lands on the student profile.

The unified data processing engine normalizes the three Mindbody surfaces into a consistent shape on the HighLevel side. The student’s HighLevel contact has one mindbody_next_session_at field, regardless of whether the next session is a class, a private, or a course meeting.

What Enrollment and Course Sync Adds to HighLevel

The student profile carries the unified view:

HighLevel contact custom fields:
- mindbody_active_enrollments        list of enrollment names
- mindbody_active_courses            list of course names
- mindbody_next_session_at           ISO timestamp (across all surfaces)
- mindbody_next_session_type         class|appointment|course|enrollment
- mindbody_next_session_instructor   string
- mindbody_next_session_location     string

Custom object: Mindbody Enrollment
- enrollment_id
- enrollment_name
- start_date / end_date
- weekly_schedule
- attendance_count / sessions_total
- status: active|completed|dropped

Custom object: Mindbody Course
- course_id
- course_name
- start_date / end_date
- meeting_dates              list of ISO timestamps
- attendance_count / meetings_total

The mindbody_next_session_at field is the unified view. It is computed across all three surfaces and updated in real time. Reminders run off that one field.

Designing One Calendar View Per Student

The HighLevel automation for reminders becomes one flow, not three:

  1. Trigger: Time-based, runs hourly against contacts with mindbody_next_session_at in the next 24 hours.
  2. Branch on mindbody_next_session_type:
  3. class: send the standard “class tonight” SMS with the instructor’s name.
  4. appointment: send the “private with [instructor]” SMS with the room/space.
  5. course: send the “Module 4 tonight, bring your notes” SMS with course-specific details.
  6. enrollment: send the “Week 5 of fundamentals” SMS.
  7. Wait until 30 minutes after session start.
  8. Branch on attendance event: present, absent, or no event yet.
  9. Absent branch: Day +2, “We missed you, do you want to switch to the Saturday slot?” SMS.

The student gets one reminder for whatever the next session actually is. Not three competing reminders for three different surfaces.

Layering Next-Appointment Tracking on Top

For students with both regular classes and recurring privates, the next-session field can flip between types within a single day. The integration handles this by always returning the earliest upcoming session across surfaces. So:

  • Wednesday morning: mindbody_next_session_at points to the 5:30 private with Professor Marco.
  • After 5:30 attendance is recorded: the field flips to the 7pm advanced class.
  • After 7pm: the field flips to Thursday morning’s enrollment session.

The owner’s automation does not have to know which surface the next session lives on. The integration computes that for them.

Computation rule (handled by CRMConnect, not by the operator):
  next_session_at = min(
    next_class_booking,
    next_appointment_booking,
    next_course_meeting,
    next_enrollment_meeting
  )

Representative Scheduling Time Saved at 180 Students

Illustrative composites, not a real customer:

  • Owner hours per week reconciling reminder schedules across class, appointment, and enrollment surfaces: roughly 3 to 5 hours, reclaimed.
  • “Why are you reminding me about a class I am not in” replies: down significantly, because the reminder respects the actual next session.
  • Private lesson attendance rate: a lift in the 4 to 7 percentage-point range, because reminders go to the right surface.
  • Enrollment drop-out rate at the mid-program mark (Week 5): down meaningfully, because attendance flow catches absent students faster.

The lift is mechanical. One source of truth for “next session” replaces three.

Builder Takeaway

  • Treat enrollments, courses, and appointments as first-class objects, not as variants of “class.” The student does not care which surface holds their session, and the CRM should not either.
  • Compute a single next_session_at field as the min across surfaces. Every downstream reminder reads from that one field.
  • Use custom objects for enrollments and courses so attendance count and program status are queryable, not just stored.
