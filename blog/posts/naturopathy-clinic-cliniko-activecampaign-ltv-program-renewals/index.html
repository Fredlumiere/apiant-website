Tracking Patient Lifetime Value and Program Renewals for a Naturopathy Clinic With Cliniko + ActiveCampaign

Fred Lumiere Jan 5, 2026 4 min read
#Healthcare#Cliniko#ActiveCampaign#CRMConnect

Naturopathy is a renewal business pretending to be a transactional one. The 12-week metabolic reset, the 6-month gut program, the 8-session adrenal support block: these are the actual revenue line. A clinic that treats each appointment as a standalone transaction will quietly leak its best patients out the back door at the end of every program.

Take an illustrative example: Greenleaf Naturopathic Wellness, a hypothetical single clinic with 3 naturopaths and around $680K in annual revenue. Most revenue comes from packages and programs sold up front. The owner cannot see in ActiveCampaign which patients have spent the most lifetime with the clinic, which are about to roll off a program, or which are good candidates for the next program in the patient journey. ActiveCampaign sees opens and clicks. It does not see the patient is two weeks from the end of their gut program with no renewal conversation scheduled.

Why off-the-shelf tools fail

ActiveCampaign on its own can tag a contact when they buy something through an ecommerce integration. It cannot track Cliniko invoices, package billing, or in-program session counts. So three things stay invisible: total lifetime spend per patient, where they are in any given program, and which programs they have completed before. Without those, renewal automations have to be triggered manually, which means they get triggered when somebody remembers, which means they get triggered too late.

A custom integration built once and never maintained tends to deliver lifetime spend as a single number that gets stale within a quarter, with no program-level context. The marketer still cannot answer “who is two weeks from the end of a program.”

The APIANT approach

CRMConnect Cliniko to ActiveCampaign syncs Cliniko invoices as ActiveCampaign deals, attaches lifetime spend to the contact, and exposes case status (active programs, milestones, session limits) so renewal automations can fire on real signal rather than a calendar guess. Cliniko remains the source of truth for what was actually billed. ActiveCampaign becomes the engine that does something useful with that truth.

Concrete steps

  1. Enable the Invoice sync to ActiveCampaign deals. Each Cliniko invoice creates a deal stamped with invoice number, issue date, amount, status, and patient. The deal feeds the lifetime spend field on the contact automatically.
  2. Confirm the lifetime spend field is populating. The custom fields you want on every contact include Lifetime Total Spend, Latest Invoice Amount, Latest Invoice Date, and Number of Invoices.
  3. Enable Case Information sync. For every patient case, capture program type (12-week metabolic reset, 6-month gut program, and so on), case status (active, on hold, closed), session limit, and sessions completed. This is the field set that powers the renewal trigger.
  4. Build LTV segmentation. Create three segments: top 10 percent of lifetime spend, mid-tier (next 40 percent), single-purchase. Each segment gets a different cadence and tone of communication.
  5. Build the program renewal automation. Trigger: a contact has an active case with a known program end date, and is 14 days from that end date, and has no booked next appointment after the program end. Action: send a renewal offer email from the treating practitioner, create a task for the practitioner to call within 3 days if no booking is made.
  6. Build the cross-sell automation. Trigger: case status moves to closed. Decision: based on what the patient just completed, recommend the logical next program. A completed metabolic reset suggests a maintenance plan. A completed gut program suggests follow-up testing and a maintenance cadence.
  7. Build the reactivation automation for high-LTV lapses. Trigger: contact is in the top 10 percent LTV segment AND has no completed appointment in 90 days. This is your most expensive group to lose, give it the most thoughtful sequence.

Outcome

A naturopathy clinic matching Greenleaf’s profile typically lifts program renewal rate from a passive 20 to 30 percent baseline into the 45 to 55 percent range within two quarters, simply by triggering the renewal conversation 14 days before program end instead of waiting until the patient noticed they stopped getting calls. Average lifetime value of the top-tier segment grows by 25 to 40 percent because cross-sell automation gets the right offer in front of the right patient at the right moment in the journey. The owner can finally answer, in real time, the question “which 20 patients should I personally call this week.”

A note on attribution honesty

Cliniko remains the source of truth for what was actually billed. ActiveCampaign deal amounts are mirrored from Cliniko invoices and should not be edited inside ActiveCampaign. If a refund or adjustment happens, it happens in Cliniko and propagates to ActiveCampaign. Anyone editing the deal amount manually in ActiveCampaign breaks the reporting silently. Lock down deal-amount editing in the ActiveCampaign user permissions if you can.

Builder takeaway

  • Program end date plus no booked next appointment is the highest-value automation trigger you can build in a package-driven practice.
  • LTV segmentation belongs on the contact, not in a static list. The integration keeps it fresh automatically.
  • Cliniko is the source of truth. ActiveCampaign is the engine. If a number disagrees, Cliniko is right.
