Accurate Next-Class Tracking in HighLevel for CrossFit On-Ramp Athletes

Fred Lumiere Oct 9, 2024 5 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#HighLevel#CRMConnect#Bi-Directional Sync

Why On-Ramp Athletes Stall After Fundamentals

The on-ramp program is the most fragile part of a CrossFit business. A new athlete completes four to six fundamentals sessions, gets cleared for regular WODs, and then disappears for three weeks. The first regular class is intimidating, the second is harder to convince yourself to book, and by the third missed week the membership is on the chopping block.

Hypothetical: Headwater CrossFit (illustrative) is a 290-member box that runs a strong fundamentals program. Their data shows that 80 percent of attrition in the first 90 days happens between the end of fundamentals and the third regular class. They use HighLevel for SMS and email automation, but the automation has no visibility into whether an on-ramp athlete actually has a next class on the books.

Why Off-the-Shelf Tools Miss This

“Next class booked” sounds simple. It is not. A reliable next-class field has to:

  • Look forward across multiple class formats (group WOD, Open Gym, skills clinics).
  • Update in real time when an athlete books or cancels.
  • Reset cleanly when a class passes (the “next class” is no longer the one that already happened).
  • Survive Mindbody schedule changes (class moved, coach swap, format change).

Most glue tools push a “class booked” event but never compute a forward-looking next_class_at attribute on the contact. The HighLevel automation has to guess from event history, which falls apart at the cancellation edge case.

The APIANT Approach

CRMConnect Mindbody to HighLevel includes Upcoming Class Bookings and Upcoming Appointment Bookings as dedicated, continuously-maintained contact fields. These are not event logs you have to query, they are live attributes that always reflect the athlete’s actual next class.

Three features carry this:

  • Comprehensive Visit and Activity Sync captures every booking and cancellation in real time.
  • Class and Appointment Calendar Sync pushes Mindbody class bookings into HighLevel calendars with accurate next-booking tracking.
  • Last Visit Tracking complements the forward-looking next-class field with a backward-looking last-visit field.

Together, every HighLevel contact has both mindbody_last_visit_at and mindbody_next_class_at always current.

What Next-Class Tracking Adds to HighLevel

The contact record carries the fields you need for branching logic:

HighLevel contact custom fields:
- mindbody_on_ramp_complete           true|false
- mindbody_last_visit_at              ISO timestamp
- mindbody_next_class_at              ISO timestamp | null
- mindbody_next_class_format          group|open_gym|skills
- mindbody_next_class_coach           string
- mindbody_visits_post_on_ramp        int

The mindbody_next_class_at field is the trigger key. When it is null and the athlete completed on-ramp, the habit-builder flow runs. When it is set, the flow waits or branches into pre-class anticipation.

Designing the Three-Class Habit-Builder Nudge

The flow runs only on athletes who finished on-ramp but have fewer than three regular-class visits:

  1. Trigger: mindbody_on_ramp_complete = true AND mindbody_visits_post_on_ramp < 3.
  2. Branch on mindbody_next_class_at:
  3. Set, within next 7 days: send a “you got this, here is the warmup” SMS the night before. End flow.
  4. Null: continue to nudge sequence.
  5. Day 1 after on-ramp completion: SMS with one-tap booking link for the next two scheduled beginner-friendly WODs.
  6. Day 3: Email from the head coach explaining how to read the next week’s programming.
  7. Day 5: SMS, “Tomorrow’s 6am has 4 spots left. Reply YES and we’ll book you in.” (Front desk handles the YES reply manually until you have a booking API in place.)
  8. Day 7: Suppress if mindbody_next_class_at is set or mindbody_visits_post_on_ramp >= 3. Otherwise hand off to coach for personal outreach.

The flow self-suppresses on every nudge based on the live next-class field. No “did they book it” check by hand. No accidentally messaging someone who already booked.

Layering in Membership Sync for Conversion

The same integration pushes membership status. Once the athlete hits three regular-class visits and is still on the on-ramp trial pricing, the conversion flow takes over:

  1. Trigger: mindbody_visits_post_on_ramp >= 3 AND mindbody_membership_status = trial.
  2. Email: “You are officially a CrossFitter.” With the membership comparison.
  3. Coach DM via the HighLevel mobile app: auto-drafted, manually sent.
  4. Membership signup conversion logged back through Mindbody to HighLevel as mindbody_membership_status = active.
  5. Suppress trial-to-member nudges automatically.

Membership status is a synced field, not a manual update. The moment the front desk converts the trial in Mindbody, HighLevel reflects it.

Representative On-Ramp-to-Member Rate at 290 Members

Illustrative composites, not a real customer:

  • On-ramp completion to first regular class baseline (no automation): roughly 55 to 65 percent.
  • With next-class-aware nudges: a lift into the 72 to 82 percent range.
  • Trial-to-full-member conversion (timed to third visit): a lift of roughly 6 to 10 percentage points.
  • Coach time spent chasing “did you book?”: down significantly, because the flow either books them or escalates honestly.

The lift comes from the timing precision. A nudge that fires when mindbody_next_class_at is null is a nudge that hits the right athlete. A blast that ignores it is a blast that annoys the athletes who already booked.

Builder Takeaway

  • Insist on a maintained next_class_at field, not an event log you query. Branching logic on a live attribute is half the engineering of branching on a derived value.
  • Suppress on the field automatically. Every nudge in the habit-builder series should check the live next-class state, not assume it.
  • Tie the trial-to-member trigger to a visit count, not a calendar date. Behavior is a better signal than days elapsed.
