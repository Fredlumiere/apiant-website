How a 3-Location Physio Clinic Closes the Loop From Facebook Ad to First Cliniko Appointment

Fred Lumiere Jan 23, 2025 4 min read
#Healthcare#Cliniko#HubSpot#CRMConnect#Bi-Directional Sync

Your marketing team can prove which ad set drove the form fill. They cannot prove which ad set drove the booked initial assessment. Those are two very different things, and only one of them pays rent.

This is the gap that quietly burns six-figure ad budgets in physio. A campaign producing 80 form fills a month looks like a winner until you discover that 60 of those people were tire-kickers and the actual booked initial assessments came mostly from a different ad set with half the form fills. Take an illustrative example: Lakeshore Physio and Sports Recovery, a hypothetical 3-location operation with 14 practitioners and around 9,000 active patients. They were spending healthy money on paid social for low back pain and post-surgical rehab. HubSpot showed cost per form fill. Cliniko showed booked initial assessments. Nothing tied them together. The marketing director allocated next month’s spend by feel.

Why off-the-shelf tools fail here

Most marketing automation stacks treat the form fill as the conversion event. That works when the product is digital. In a physio clinic the actual conversion is the patient showing up for their initial assessment 9 to 14 days after the form fill. By the time the appointment is booked, then arrives, then gets billed, the original campaign context is long gone in HubSpot.

The usual workarounds are all bad. Manual tagging by the front desk gets done on Tuesday and forgotten on Wednesday. A weekly CSV export from Cliniko into HubSpot creates duplicate contacts, breaks deduplication, and lags by 5 to 7 days. A “marketing source” picklist in Cliniko that practitioners are supposed to fill in turns into “Other / Unknown” 70 percent of the time. None of these survive contact with a real clinic schedule.

The APIANT approach

CRMConnect for Cliniko and HubSpot solves the attribution problem at the data layer instead of the workflow layer. The integration runs a bi-directional patient profile sync, mirrors Cliniko Appointments as HubSpot deals on a dedicated Appointments Pipeline, and maps Cliniko invoices to a separate Invoices Pipeline. Because the deal stages reflect the actual Cliniko appointment status (Booked, Arrived, Did Not Arrive, Cancelled), the original HubSpot contact source carries all the way through to billed revenue without a human in the middle.

The bi-directional piece is non-negotiable for attribution. When the front desk merges duplicate patients in Cliniko, or updates contact info, the change propagates to HubSpot rather than orphaning the original source data on a deleted record.

Concrete steps

  1. Switch on CRMConnect Cliniko to HubSpot and run the initial patient sync. Confirm that HubSpot original source, original source drill-down 1, and original source drill-down 2 are populated on every contact that came through a HubSpot form.
  2. Enable the Appointments Pipeline. Each Cliniko appointment becomes a HubSpot deal stamped with the patient, the practitioner, the appointment type, and the business location. The deal stages mirror Cliniko statuses: Booked, Arrived, Did Not Arrive, Cancelled.
  3. Map Appointment Deal Amount from the appointment’s expected billable item. For an initial assessment priced at $180, every Booked deal lands with a deal amount of $180, every Arrived deal converts that to recognized revenue.
  4. Set up a HubSpot report grouped by original source drill-down 1 (your campaign or ad set name), with metrics for deals created, deals in Arrived stage, and total deal amount in Arrived stage. This is your real cost-per-revenue-dollar report.
  5. For deeper analysis, layer in the Invoices Pipeline. Each Cliniko invoice becomes a HubSpot deal with the actual billed amount, net of discounts and adjustments. Now your campaign report can show booked revenue (Appointments Pipeline, Arrived) and billed revenue (Invoices Pipeline, Paid).

Outcome

Working with the illustrative numbers above, Lakeshore would typically discover within the first 60 days that the Instagram low back pain creative they thought was their best performer was actually third by cost-per-arrived-assessment, and that a quieter Facebook video remarketing audience was producing initial assessments at roughly 60 percent of the cost. Reallocating spend on that signal alone tends to move blended cost per booked initial assessment from the $140 to $160 range down into the $90 to $110 range within a quarter. None of that requires more ad spend, only spend pointed in the right direction.

Builder takeaway

  • Treat the booked and arrived appointment as the real conversion event, not the form fill. Build the Cliniko Appointments Pipeline in HubSpot first, then layer reporting on top of it.
  • Insist on bi-directional patient sync. One-way pushes break the moment the front desk merges or edits a Cliniko record, and they take attribution with them.
  • Keep the source of truth where it belongs. Original source stays with HubSpot, appointment status stays with Cliniko, billed amounts stay with Cliniko invoices. The integration just keeps them in lockstep.
