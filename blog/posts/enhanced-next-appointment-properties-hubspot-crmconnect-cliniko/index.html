See Every Patient's Next Cliniko Appointment Inside HubSpot

Fred Lumiere Oct 11, 2024 3 min read
#Cliniko#HubSpot#Healthcare#CRMConnect

In a healthcare practice, the quality of care your team delivers depends on how well they know each patient. Not just what happened last visit, but what is coming up next. If a patient finished a major procedure and has nothing booked for the follow-up, someone needs to notice and act on that.

CRMConnect: Cliniko to HubSpot, built by APIANT, has always kept patient appointments visible in HubSpot. The latest update makes that view complete. Your team can now see each patient’s upcoming appointment alongside their past visits, all on the patient’s record in HubSpot, without opening Cliniko.

What’s new

Until now, the patient record in HubSpot gave your team a clear picture of past visits: what the patient had done and when. The missing piece was the future.

This update fills it in. Every patient record in HubSpot now also shows their next appointment: when it is, what it is for, which practitioner they are seeing, and where. Paired with the visit history that was already there, your team gets the full story of each patient at a glance. When they last came in, what they came in for, and what they have booked next.

Just as important, when an appointment is cancelled and the patient has nothing else booked, the upcoming-visit details clear automatically. Your team never has to second-guess whether what they are looking at is current. If HubSpot shows an upcoming appointment, it is real.

Why this matters for your practice

Having past and upcoming visits side by side, in the system your team already works in, changes how the day runs.

Better patient relationships. When a staff member knows a patient’s history and what is coming up, every conversation lands better. Reminders are timely, follow-ups are relevant, and patients feel looked after rather than processed.

Less switching, fewer mistakes. Your team can manage schedules, follow-ups, and patient communication without bouncing between Cliniko and HubSpot. And because the appointment details update on their own, there is no manual re-keying, which means no typos and no stale information.

Decisions based on accurate data. Everyone on the team sees the same current picture. That consistency is what lets your front desk, your practitioners, and your marketing work from the same facts.

How your team can put this to work

Catching patients who need a follow-up

With both the last and next visit in view, your staff can spot the gaps. If a patient finished a significant procedure and has not booked their follow-up, that stands out immediately. Your team can reach out with a friendly reminder or simply offer to book the next visit for them, before that patient slips away.

Preparing patients properly

Practitioners can look at a patient’s history and their upcoming appointment together and plan ahead. If the next visit is a procedure that needs preparation, staff can send the right instructions well in advance. Better-prepared patients mean smoother visits and better outcomes.

Smarter outreach

Your marketing or front-desk team can group patients by where they stand. Patients who have not booked a new appointment are an obvious group to reach with a gentle re-engagement message, perhaps a reminder about a service they have had before or a relevant offer. Instead of one generic blast, every message fits where the patient actually is.

What this means for your business

The enhanced upcoming-appointment view in CRMConnect: Cliniko to HubSpot, built by APIANT, gives your practice a real advantage in managing patient relationships. Cliniko appointment information stays visible and current in HubSpot, so your team can deliver more personal care, follow up reliably, and run a tighter operation.
