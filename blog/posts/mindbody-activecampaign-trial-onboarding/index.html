Building a First-Class Welcome Sequence in ActiveCampaign From Mindbody Bookings

Fred Lumiere Dec 29, 2025 5 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#ActiveCampaign#CRMConnect#Bi-Directional Sync

A new student books their first trial class at 8:42 p.m. on a Tuesday. They picked Wednesday’s 6 p.m. Intermediate Jazz with Ms. Renata. They have never set foot in a dance studio as an adult before.

The ideal sequence looks like this. Tuesday night, 9 p.m.: a short welcome email confirming the booking and naming the instructor by first name. Wednesday morning, 10 a.m.: a “what to wear, where to park, where the entrance is” email. Wednesday at 4 p.m.: a quick text-style email reminder. Wednesday at 9 p.m. (post-class): a “how did it go” note from Ms. Renata personally. Friday morning: a “ready for class two” booking nudge.

That sequence wins trials. The studio that sends it converts trial-to-member at 40+ percent. The studio that sends “Welcome to our studio!” twice and a generic discount converts at 12 percent.

Hypothetical: Lumen Movement Dance (illustrative), a single-location dance studio with 410 active students mixed across kids and adults, was firmly in the 12 percent camp. ActiveCampaign was set up. The “welcome series” was a generic 4-email drip. It did not know which class anyone booked.

Why Last-Visit Date Alone Isn’t Enough

Most Mindbody-to-ActiveCampaign setups stop at syncing the contact and a last-visit date. That gets you a generic “thanks for visiting” email, which is barely better than the generic welcome.

The missing pieces are everything that makes the email feel like it was written for that student:

  • Which specific class did they book?
  • Who is the instructor (by first name)?
  • What time is the class?
  • Did they actually show up?
  • Have they booked the next one?

Without those fields, every personalization token reads “your recent class.” The student knows generic when they see it.

A naive Zapier-from-Mindbody build can technically push some of these, but you end up writing per-class-type filter logic that breaks every time the studio adds a new offering. And there is no clean way to handle “what did they book next” because that requires polling.

The Fields CRMConnect Populates on Booking

CRMConnect Mindbody to ActiveCampaign creates and populates 120+ custom fields in ActiveCampaign on day one, specifically for wellness use cases. The relevant fields for a first-class sequence include:

  • last_visit_date, last_visit_type, last_visit_staff, last_visit_service, last_visit_event_name
  • next_scheduled_appointment (the upcoming class booking sync)
  • intro_offer_purchased, intro_offer_purchase_date
  • total_visits, remaining_sessions, membership_status

From the app page: “Instantly sync clients’ upcoming class details, enabling perfectly timed reminders and personalized communications.” Profiles sync instantly, and bookings, visits, and sales post within seconds.

The booking event looks roughly like this on the ActiveCampaign contact, the moment they confirm:

{
  "email": "student@example.com",
  "intro_offer_purchased": "5-Class Intro Pack",
  "next_scheduled_appointment_date": "2026-05-20T18:00:00",
  "next_scheduled_appointment_service": "Intermediate Jazz",
  "next_scheduled_appointment_staff": "Renata",
  "next_scheduled_appointment_location": "Lumen Studio A",
  "total_visits": 0,
  "membership_status": "Intro Active"
}

That is enough to drive a sequence that does not feel like spam.

Building the 5-Touch Welcome Automation

In ActiveCampaign (the deal-pipeline based touches need Plus or higher; SMS needs Professional), build one automation with five steps, enrolled on intro_offer_purchased changes AND next_scheduled_appointment_date is in the future.

Touch 1: hour 0, booking confirmation. Personalized with next_scheduled_appointment_staff and next_scheduled_appointment_service. “Hey [first name], you’re booked for [service] with [staff first name] on [day]. Stoked to have you in.”

Touch 2: morning of, 8 hours before class. Logistics email. What to wear, parking, entry door, what to bring. This is where studios under-deliver; first-class students are nervous about practical details, not technique.

Touch 3: 2 hours before class. Short reminder, calendar-style language. “See you at 6 p.m.” Almost no content. High open rate, drives walk-in confidence.

Touch 4: 2 hours after class, conditional. Branch on last_visit_date is today. If true: thank-you note “from” the instructor, with a one-line ask for honest feedback. If false (they no-showed): a soft “we missed you” with a one-click reschedule link.

Touch 5: 36 hours after class. Booking nudge for class two. Pre-suggest a class with the same instructor (use last_visit_staff to drive the suggestion). This is the highest-leverage email in the sequence; second-class booking inside 7 days is the single best predictor of trial-to-member conversion.

Use the Mindbody Appointment Pipeline that CRMConnect creates in ActiveCampaign as the deal pipeline backing this. Each appointment becomes a deal with status, schedule changes, and remaining sessions tracked. From the app page: “Automatically create a live appointment pipeline in ActiveCampaign, clearly tracking booking statuses, schedule changes, and remaining sessions.”

The pipeline lets your studio manager scan all booked first-time students at a glance and personally welcome the ones whose first class is tomorrow. Small, but it lifts conversion measurably.

Representative Trial-to-Member Conversion at 410 Students

In a representative deployment at studios of this scale, trial-to-member conversion typically moves from baseline 12 to 18 percent into the 28 to 35 percent range within 60 days of going live with a sequence like this. The biggest contributors are touch 4 (the post-class instructor email) and touch 5 (the second-class booking nudge). Touch 3 (the 2-hour-before reminder) reduces no-show rates by roughly 25 to 35 percent, which is most of where the math really comes from; you cannot convert a trial that did not happen.

For Lumen Movement specifically, with roughly 30 to 40 trial bookings a month at a $99/month membership, lifting conversion by 15 points adds roughly 5 net new members per month, or about $5,900 in annualized MRR off a single automation.

These numbers are illustrative; per-class instructor quality is the dominant variable, and no email sequence can save trials at a studio with a weak first-class experience.

Builder Takeaway

  • What to copy: the 5-touch structure, especially touch 3 (the short pre-class reminder) and touch 5 (the instructor-matched second-class nudge). Both feel like extra work to write and they are not; one paragraph each.
  • What to watch for: ActiveCampaign deal pipelines and richer automation features need Plus or higher; SMS needs Professional. CRMConnect syncs the data but you build the automation. Also note: Mindbody clients without an email get a unique placeholder email written for them so every client syncs cleanly into ActiveCampaign, but you do not want to mail-blast those placeholders, so segment them out.
  • When this pattern doesn’t fit: drop-in class businesses where students never intend to come back as members (think tourist-heavy yoga in a vacation town) get less out of the conversion-focused touches. Keep touches 1 to 4, drop touch 5.
