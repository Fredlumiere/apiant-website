Turn Mindbody Intro Offers Into a 7-Day HubSpot Nurture That Books Class Two

Fred Lumiere Oct 3, 2024 5 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#HubSpot#CRMConnect#Bi-Directional Sync

A new student walks in, swipes their card on a $39 intro-offer, takes their first class, and walks out happy. Then the front desk gets slammed for the next four hours, and nobody touches HubSpot until the end of the shift. By the time someone exports the day’s Mindbody clients to CSV and uploads them into HubSpot the next morning, that student is roughly 20 hours into the most important window they will ever spend in your funnel: the first 14 days of an intro offer.

Hypothetical: Lotus & Lattice Yoga (illustrative), a single-location boutique studio with 280 active members and 14 weekly classes, watched this happen every week. Their intro-to-member conversion sat in the high teens. The owner was convinced the front desk just needed to “be faster,” but the front desk was already at capacity. The problem was not effort. The problem was that no nurture sequence could fire until a human typed the lead into HubSpot.

Why a CSV Export From Mindbody Each Morning Doesn’t Work

The CSV workaround has three quiet failures.

First, it is asynchronous. The earliest a sequence can fire is the next business day, which means the “thanks for your first class” email arrives 18 to 36 hours after the actual class. By then the student has either booked class two on their own or moved on.

Second, the CSV does not carry behavior. You get a name, email, and a purchase line. You do not get which instructor they took, which class style, which time slot, or whether they booked their next visit. That means every email is generic.

Third, the CSV does not handle the round trip. If a HubSpot form captures a lead who later buys an intro pack in Mindbody, you now have two records (one in each system) and no clean way to merge their behavior.

Zapier and similar task-based tools can move the new client object in real time, but they fall apart when you need the rest of the picture: visit history, last-visit date, upcoming class booking, remaining sessions on the intro pack. Mapping all of that by hand is a multi-week Zap project, and every Mindbody schema change breaks it.

What CRMConnect Actually Syncs on Day Zero

CRMConnect Mindbody to HubSpot is a turnkey API App. The instant the intro offer is purchased, the integration fires:

  • Two-way client profile sync. The Mindbody client is created or matched in HubSpot, with smart duplicate detection so a prior HubSpot lead with the same first/last/email gets updated, not duplicated.
  • 120+ custom HubSpot properties are pre-created and populated. These are not generic placeholders; they are wellness-specific fields like total visits, last visit date, last visit type, last visit staff, last visit service, next scheduled appointment, intro offer purchased, membership status, remaining sessions.
  • Comprehensive activity sync. Every booking, visit, missed class, and purchase is reflected in HubSpot in near real time. Profiles sync instantly. Bookings, visits, and sales post within seconds. The Mindbody Sales Pipeline reconciles daily.
  • Mindbody Appointment Pipeline is created in HubSpot as a deal pipeline. Each appointment is a deal with status, service, staff, and remaining sessions.

The day-zero payload for a new intro buyer looks roughly like this on the HubSpot contact:

{
  "email": "student@example.com",
  "intro_offer_purchased": "30 Days Unlimited",
  "intro_offer_purchase_date": "2026-05-17",
  "last_visit_date": "2026-05-17",
  "last_visit_service": "Vinyasa Flow",
  "last_visit_staff": "Priya",
  "next_scheduled_appointment": null,
  "total_visits": 1,
  "remaining_sessions": null,
  "membership_status": "Intro Offer Active"
}

That is enough to drive a real sequence.

Building the HubSpot Workflow: First Class, Second Class, Conversion

In HubSpot Pro (workflows require Pro), build one workflow enrolled on intro_offer_purchased is known AND intro_offer_purchase_date in last 1 day.

  • Hour 2 after first class: Auto-enroll the contact in a “thanks, here is what to expect” sequence personalized with last_visit_service and last_visit_staff. This goes out the same evening, not the next morning.
  • Day 2: If next_scheduled_appointment is still null, send a “pick your second class” email with a Mindbody booking link. This is the single biggest lever; the conversion gap between members who attend class two within 7 days and those who do not is brutal.
  • Day 4: Branch on total_visits. If still at 1, send a “your first week” email with the instructor’s bio. If at 2 or more, send a “you’re building momentum” email instead.
  • Day 12: Trigger the membership offer 48 hours before intro expiry. Personalize with total_visits so the language matches their actual engagement (a 6-visit student gets a different pitch than a 1-visit student).

Representative Numbers After 90 Days

In a representative deployment at studios of similar scale to Lotus & Lattice, this pattern moves intro-to-member conversion from roughly 18 percent to 26 to 30 percent within a quarter. Most of the lift comes from the day-2 booking nudge, which compresses the time-to-second-class from a typical 9 days down to under 4. Email open rates on the day-zero “thanks” email tend to land north of 60 percent because the message arrives while the student still has the studio fresh in mind.

Front-desk time spent on data entry drops to effectively zero, because the round trip is automated. If a HubSpot form captures someone who later walks into class, CRMConnect’s duplicate logic merges them. If a member updates their email in HubSpot, the “Push to Mindbody” property pushes it back to the right site.

These numbers are illustrative; your conversion baseline and your nurture copy will dominate the outcome.

Builder Takeaway

  • What to copy: the three-touch structure (hour-2 thanks, day-2 second-class nudge, day-12 conversion). The day-2 nudge is the highest-leverage email in the sequence and the one most studios skip.
  • What to watch for: HubSpot workflows need HubSpot Pro Hub. Free Hub will accept the synced data but cannot fire automations on it. Mindbody also imposes API rate limits; the default sync window covers 7 days back and 30 days forward for appointments and 1 month back for sales, which is fine for new intros but matters if you backfill historical clients.
  • When this pattern doesn’t fit: very high-volume class businesses with thousands of weekly intros may want a Sales Hub Enterprise tier to auto-enroll in sequences from workflows, rather than fan-out via separate emails. For very small studios under 50 weekly intros, a manual workflow with HubSpot Free plus tasks may actually be cheaper than the integration tier.
