Rolling Up 5 Mindbody Properties Into One Zoho CRM Revenue View

Fred Lumiere Nov 24, 2025 5 min read
#Fitness & Wellness#Mindbody#Zoho CRM#CRMConnect#For Enterprise

The Multi-Property Reporting Mess

Wellness groups with multiple retreat properties run each location as its own Mindbody site, often in different currencies, often with overlapping client bases. The corporate office needs one number: total revenue across all properties, in a base currency, with the ability to drill down to any single property without leaving the dashboard. The reality is usually five spreadsheets, one currency conversion sheet, and a weekly recurring panic.

Hypothetical: Heliotrope Wellness Group (illustrative) is a five-property retreat operator with a central Zoho CRM organization. Properties sit in three currencies. The group COO opens five Mindbody reports every Monday and rebuilds the consolidated view from scratch. The exchange rates are last week’s. The revenue numbers do not match the bank deposits. Decisions on cross-property marketing and shared retreat programming get postponed because the data is never trustworthy enough to act on.

Why Off-the-Shelf Tools Fail at This

Multi-property roll-up requires four things that most generic tools skip:

  • Per-site revenue capture preserving the site dimension end-to-end.
  • Currency-aware data with the original currency captured and a base currency convertible at query time.
  • Unified client identity across properties (a guest at Property A and Property B is one client, not two).
  • Property-level drill-down without losing the consolidated view.

The unified identity is the hardest part. A guest who books at three properties under slightly different email casings or with different last-name spellings will end up as three records in any system that does not have smart matching.

The APIANT Approach

CRMConnect Mindbody to Zoho CRM is built with multi-location support and comprehensive revenue visibility as core features. From the API App page: “Comprehensive Revenue Visibility: Clearly link client payments and purchases to services, campaigns, and locations.”

The features that carry the multi-property model:

  • Multi-Location Support for syncing many Mindbody sites into one Zoho CRM organization.
  • Comprehensive Purchases and Payments capturing every transaction with site and currency intact.
  • Two-Way Client Profile Sync with smart duplicate handling for cross-property identity.
  • Sales Performance Dashboard as the consolidated view, with native filters for drill-down.
  • Custom Object Mapping so the per-site, per-currency data is queryable.

The unified data processing engine handles the format and currency dimension cleanly. Each transaction lands with the original currency captured. The base currency conversion happens at query time using the rate the group sets.

What Comprehensive Revenue Sync Means in Zoho CRM

The data model on the Zoho side:

Zoho CRM custom objects:
- MB Sales
    - sale_id, site_id, currency, total_local, total_base
    - sale_date_utc, sale_date_local
    - contact_id, payment_status
- MB Sale Line Items
    - line_item_id, sale_id, site_id, sku, category, qty
    - price_local, price_base
- MB Payments
    - payment_id, site_id, currency, amount_local, amount_base
    - method, status, processed_at_utc

Contact-level fields:
- mindbody_visited_properties        list of site_ids
- mindbody_home_property             primary property
- mindbody_lifetime_revenue_base     converted to base currency
- mindbody_property_revenue          map of site_id -> revenue_base

Every monetary field is captured in both the local currency and the base currency. The conversion is computed once at sync time and re-runs daily as exchange rates update. The corporate view reads total_base. The property GMs read total_local.

Mapping Sites and Currencies to a Single Roll-Up

The configuration is one-time and stable:

Site configuration:
site-01 (Costa Rica)    currency: USD  -> base: USD
site-02 (Bali)          currency: IDR  -> base: USD (FX rate)
site-03 (Tulum)         currency: MXN  -> base: USD (FX rate)
site-04 (Sedona)        currency: USD  -> base: USD
site-05 (Provence)      currency: EUR  -> base: USD (FX rate)

The FX rate is sourced from the group’s preferred rate provider (a daily ECB feed, a bank rate, or whatever audit standard the group follows). The conversion is applied at sync time and stored on the record, so historical reports do not shift when today’s rate moves.

The corporate dashboard then displays:

  • Total group revenue this month: Sum of total_base across all MB Sales.
  • Per-property contribution: Sum of total_base grouped by site_id, with a percentage of total.
  • MoM and YoY deltas at both the group and per-property level.

Per-Property Drill-Downs Without Duplicate Records

The cross-property guest is the hardest case to get right. Smart duplicate handling matches on email, phone, and name across sites and merges to a single Zoho contact. The contact then carries:

  • mindbody_visited_properties as a list of all site_ids the guest has booked.
  • mindbody_property_revenue as a map of site_id to local revenue.
  • mindbody_lifetime_revenue_base as the converted total.

This lets corporate ask “how many guests have stayed at multiple properties this year” and get an honest answer. The number is usually meaningful, and the answer drives cross-property marketing programs that did not exist before.

Each property GM gets their own filtered view of the same data: their site’s sales, their site’s bookings, their site’s repeat guests. The GM does not see other properties’ numbers, but corporate does.

Representative Operating Decisions at 5 Properties

Illustrative composites, not a real customer:

  • COO hours per week on consolidated reporting: cut from roughly 6 to 8, down to a same-day dashboard read.
  • Currency conversion errors in the consolidated report: eliminated, because conversion happens at sync, not in a spreadsheet.
  • Cross-property guest segment surfaced for the first time: typically 8 to 15 percent of the active client base, a real cross-sell pool.
  • Operating decisions traceable to the dashboard in the first quarter: typically 4 to 6 (one property pricing review, one cross-property package launch, one underperforming-property staffing change, one marketing channel reallocation).

The lift is not “more revenue this quarter.” It is “weekly decisions based on real data, not Friday’s spreadsheet.”

Builder Takeaway

  • Capture both local and base currency at sync time. Re-converting at query time using today’s rate destroys historical accuracy.
  • Use smart matching across sites for cross-property identity. The cross-property segment is usually larger than corporate thinks, and it is a real marketing channel.
  • Give property GMs a filtered view of the same dataset corporate sees. One data source, two permission levels, beats two data sources every time.
